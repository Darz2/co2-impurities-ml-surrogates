# TabPFN — Timing & Cost Notes

Working notes for the timing/cost analysis (basis for the Overleaf table).

- **Hardware:** Snellius `gpu_h100` partition (1 node, NVIDIA H100). cDFT is CPU/BLAS-threaded; TabPFN is GPU.
- **Env:** `.A6` (Python 3.13) — Jupyter kernel registered as `py_a6`.
- **Two studies:**
  1. **cDFT + PC-SAFT vs TabPFN** — physics engine vs surrogate on the same γ map (speed-up + accuracy). ✅ done.
  2. **TabPFN variants micro-benchmark** — training / inference / RAM / model-size for `without_HPO | with_HPO | RF+TabPFN`. ⏳ running (`benchmark_timing.ipynb`).

## 1. cDFT + PC-SAFT  vs  with-HPO TabPFN  — γ map  ✅

- cDFT γ maps generated by `SENSITIVITY_ANALYSIS/VLE_IFT_V5.ipynb` (`feos`/BLAS, CPU, ThreadPool), ~10k (T,P,z) points per feed (composition). Map time = papermill `duration` of each per-feed run (`1716414/1716414_i/`).
- ML side: saved **with-HPO TabPFN γ** model predicts the **identical** points (`cdft_vs_ml_bundle/`, runs on H100, inference-only).
- Accuracy (R²/RMSE) computed against the true cDFT γ on those same points.

| feed | points | cDFT map [s] | ML map [s] | speed-up | R² | RMSE [mN/m] |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0 | 9 987  | 7 481.1  (2.1 h)  | 6.89 | 1 086× | 0.99997 | 0.035 |
| 1 | 9 764  | 19 253.2 (5.3 h)  | 5.73 | 3 359× | 0.99998 | 0.026 |
| 2 | 10 120 | 18 286.8 (5.1 h)  | 3.56 | 5 136× | 0.99995 | 0.046 |
| 3 | 9 910  | 65 151.9 (18.1 h) | 4.34 | 15 026× | 0.99997 | 0.032 |
| 4 | 10 050 | 63 255.5 (17.6 h) | 3.54 | 17 874× | 0.99996 | 0.038 |

**Takeaways**
- **Speed-up ≈ 10³–10⁴×**: cDFT 2–18 h/map vs surrogate 3.5–6.9 s (~0.35–0.69 ms/point vs 0.75–6.6 s/point for cDFT).
- **Accuracy essentially exact**: R² ≈ 0.9999+, RMSE ≈ 0.03–0.05 mN/m → speed-up at no meaningful accuracy cost.
- Speed-up grows with feed because cDFT cost varies a lot while ML inference is ~constant.

**Files:** `cdft_vs_ml_bundle/cdft_vs_ml_timing.csv`, `…/cdft_vs_ml_table.tex`, `…/cdft_vs_ml_timing.ipynb` (portable, self-contained).

## Caveats (for the paper text)

- **cDFT map time** = the *whole* `VLE_IFT_V5` notebook (envelope + cDFT sweep + plots) → slight **over**estimate of the pure γ-sweep cost.
- **ML map time** = warm `predict` (model-load excluded; reported separately).
- **Micro-benchmark labels:** "Training GPU time" = `cuda.Event` window across `.fit` (GPU-active wall, since fit interleaves CPU+GPU); "Used RAM" = RSS rise during `.fit` (in-process, freed between combos).
- **RF+TabPFN "P_bubble"** hung at ~48 767 s (~13.5 h) in two separate runs (GPU-0 stall) — treat as an outlier; siblings are ~5 000 s.
- The headline comparison is inherently **CPU-cDFT (hours) vs GPU-ML (ms)** — i.e. the deployment trade-off.
